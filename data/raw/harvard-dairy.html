Milk and Dairy Choices

Publisher: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Year: 2024.

Calcium and Dairy Products

Calcium is essential for bone and tooth health, muscle contraction, blood clotting, and nerve function. The recommended dietary allowance (RDA) is generally 1,000 mg to 1,200 mg daily depending on age and sex.

Guidelines and Consumption

While some guidelines recommend three servings of dairy daily, research on this has produced conflicting results. For adults and children over 9, there is no strict requirement to consume large amounts of milk; one or two glasses per day is generally considered sufficient.

Those who consume less milk can obtain necessary calcium from other sources such as leafy greens, fortified foods, and supplements.

While early guidelines favored low-fat or non-fat dairy to reduce saturated fat intake, more recent research suggests that full-fat dairy may be just as healthful. Fermented foods like yogurt and cheese offer additional benefits, such as improved digestibility and lower lactose content.
