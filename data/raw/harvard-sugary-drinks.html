Sugary Drinks and Sugar-Sweetened Beverages

Publisher: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Year: 2024.

The Impact of Sugary Drinks

Sugar-sweetened beverages (SSBs) or sugary drinks are leading sources of added sugars in the diet. This includes regular soda, fruit drinks, sports drinks, energy drinks, and sweetened waters.

Frequent consumption of sugary drinks is strongly associated with weight gain, obesity, type 2 diabetes, heart disease, kidney diseases, non-alcoholic liver disease, and tooth decay and cavities.

What to Drink Instead

Water is the best choice for quenching your thirst. Coffee and tea, without added sweeteners, are also healthy choices. Some people enjoy carbonated water with a splash of 100% fruit juice as a healthier alternative to soda.

Recommendations

It is recommended to limit or completely avoid sugary drinks. Replacing just one sugary drink per day with water can significantly reduce your daily calorie and sugar intake, leading to better long-term health outcomes.
