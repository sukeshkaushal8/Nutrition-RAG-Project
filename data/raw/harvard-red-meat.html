Protein: Limiting Red and Processed Meat

Publisher: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Year: 2024.

Protein and Health

Protein is a vital macronutrient composed of amino acids. While the National Academy of Medicine suggests 10% to 35% of daily calories from protein, the *source* of that protein is critical for long-term health. It is important to consider the entire "package" that comes with your protein, including fats, fiber, and other nutrients.

Limit Red and Processed Meats

Research indicates that higher intake of red and processed meats (like bacon, sausage, and hot dogs) is associated with an increased risk of mortality. The Nutrition Source consistently advises limiting red meat consumption and avoiding processed meats entirely.

Choose Healthy Protein Sources

Conversely, plant-based proteins—such as nuts, beans, legumes, and whole grains—are linked to lower risks of heart disease and other health issues.

  • Prioritize: Fish, poultry, beans, and nuts.
  • Reduce: Meat and cheese portions.
  • Shift the Focus: Try using meat as a flavoring or side dish rather than the "center of attention" on your plate.
