Healthy Fats and Cholesterol

Publisher: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Year: 2024.

Shift in Dietary Understanding

Contrary to outdated advice promoting low-fat diets, current research emphasizes that the *type* of fat consumed is more important than total fat intake. Low-fat diets often replace healthy fats with refined carbohydrates (e.g., white flour, sugary drinks). These "fast carbs" can spike blood sugar, increase triglycerides, and lower "good" HDL cholesterol.

Healthy Fats (Unsaturated Fats)

Found in liquid vegetable oils, nuts, and seeds, unsaturated fats are considered beneficial. They can improve blood cholesterol levels, reduce inflammation, and help stabilize heart rhythms.

Practical Recommendations

  • Choose unsaturated fats: Prioritize plant-based oils like olive and canola for cooking and baking.
  • Avoid trans fats: Read labels to ensure products do not contain partially hydrogenated oils.
  • Focus on quality: Incorporate sources like avocado, salmon, nuts, and seeds as part of a balanced diet.
