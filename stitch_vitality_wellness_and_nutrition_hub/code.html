How does food pairing influence non-heme iron and plant protein absorption? What should I eat with lentils or spinach?
smart_toy
NutriVibe AI Dietitian psychology 4 Sources Cited

Pairing legumes (like lentils) or dark leafy greens (like spinach) with specific micronutrients alters their biological availability significantly. Plant-based non-heme iron has a baseline absorption rate of only 2% to 20% due to binding phytates and polyphenols.

science Clinical Synergy Rules:
  • check_circle Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C): Adding 100mg of Vitamin C (e.g. lemon juice, bell peppers) quadruples non-heme iron reduction to ferrous Fe²⁺ form for intestinal mucosal uptake.
  • check_circle Limiting Amino Acid Complementarity: Lentils are deficient in methionine/cysteine but rich in lysine. Pairing with seeds, rice, or tahini produces a DIAAS (Digestible Indispensable Amino Acid Score) comparable to animal protein.
  • warning Avoid Tannin Co-Ingestion: Coffee and black tea polyphenols consumed within 60 minutes decrease iron uptake by up to 60-70%.
